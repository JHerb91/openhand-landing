Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

OpenHand ("OpenHand," "we," "us") is operated by The Arc-Hives, LLC. This policy explains what information we collect through the OpenHand app, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect the information described below directly from you as you use the app.

  • Account information: the email address and password you use to sign in.
  • Profile information: your name, age, gender, who you're interested in matching with, a short bio, occupation, the city you enter, your photos, your preferred match age range and distance, and lifestyle answers you choose to share (such as religious affiliation, and whether you smoke, drink, or use substances).
  • Compatibility information: your answers to our personality assessment and relationship-values questionnaire ("Giving Profile"), and the scores generated from them.
  • Messages: the content of conversations you have with matches inside the app.
  • Match feedback: reasons you provide when declining a suggested match, used to improve future suggestions.
  • Approximate location: the city you type in is converted to an approximate latitude/longitude so we can filter matches by distance. We do not access your device's precise GPS location.
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active OpenHand Pro subscription, and its renewal date. Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never receive your card number.
  • Push notification token: a device identifier used only to deliver notifications (e.g., new match, new message).

How We Use Information

  • To generate compatibility-based match suggestions and explain why you and another member were matched.
  • To operate in-app messaging between matched members.
  • To manage your subscription and unlock Pro features.
  • To send notifications you'd expect (new matches, new messages).
  • To maintain the safety and integrity of the platform, including reviewing reported conduct.

We do not use your information for third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information. OpenHand contains no ad network or advertising SDK of any kind.

Other members you match with can see the profile information, photos, and messages you choose to share with them inside the app. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Export your data: from Settings, you can request an export of your profile, personality, messages, and match data at any time.
  • Delete your account: from Settings, you can permanently delete your account. This removes your profile, personality and relationship data, matches, and messages from our systems.
  • Correct your information: you can edit your profile at any time from within the app.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, personality/relationship data, matches, and messages are deleted from our active systems; residual copies may briefly persist in backups before they are overwritten.

Children's Privacy

OpenHand is intended for adults only. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit and database access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to openhandapp@gmail.com.
